Fair Housing Notice

Equal Housing Opportunity

Bagley Vision Lofts is committed to compliance with all federal, state, and local fair housing laws. We do not discriminate against any person because of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status, disability, or any other characteristic protected under applicable law.

Our Commitment

All residents and prospective residents will be treated equally and fairly. We are dedicated to providing housing opportunities to all qualified applicants on equal terms, and we welcome and encourage applications from everyone.

Our leasing criteria are applied consistently to all applicants. Housing decisions, including those related to rental terms, conditions, and privileges, are based solely on legitimate business considerations such as creditworthiness, rental history, and ability to meet lease obligations.

Reasonable Accommodations

We are committed to providing reasonable accommodations and modifications for individuals with disabilities in accordance with the Fair Housing Act and applicable state and local laws. If you require a reasonable accommodation or modification, please contact us and we will work with you to address your needs.

Filing a Complaint

If you believe you have been the victim of housing discrimination, you may file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) by calling 1-800-669-9777 (voice) or 1-800-927-9275 (TTY), or by visiting www.hud.gov. You may also file a complaint with the Michigan Department of Civil Rights at 1-800-482-3604.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our fair housing practices, please contact us at info@2150Bagley.com.
